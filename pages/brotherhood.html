Our Legacy

A History of Excellence

Lambda Chi Alpha was founded on November 2, 1909, at Boston University by Warren A. Cole. From those humble beginnings, we have grown into one of the largest and most respected fraternities in North America, with over 300,000 initiated members.

The Zeta Rho Chapter at Florida State University carries this proud tradition forward in Tallahassee. Our chapter has produced four FSU Student Body Presidents, NFL legends like Fred Biletnikoff and Ron Sellers, and countless leaders in business, government, and the arts.

In Spring 2025, Lambda Chi Alpha returned to Florida State with renewed vigor. Now in 2026, with two classes of initiated brothers and a third class on their journey to initiation, we continue building on our legacy of excellence, brotherhood, and leadership at FSU.

Our Home

The Chapter House

Moving to Heritage Grove in Fall 2026!

Built specifically for Lambda Chi Alpha in 2005 at a cost of approximately $3.7 million, our chapter house stands as a testament to the strength of our brotherhood and the generosity of our alumni.

The property consists of two separate buildings connected by an expansive courtyard featuring a half-court basketball court, putting green, and fire pit—perfect for brotherhood bonding.

  • Three-story front building with 21 apartments
  • Two-story chapter house with 8 additional apartments
  • Grand chapter room for meetings and ceremonies
  • Covered patio area and indoor fireplace lounge
  • Courtyard with half-court, putting green, and fire pit

Chapter Life

What We Do

Our brotherhood extends far beyond social events. We're committed to developing well-rounded men.

Academic Excellence

We maintain high academic standards with study rooms at the library multiple times a week and tutoring to ensure every brother succeeds.

Community Service

Through our partnerships with the Movember Foundation, Folds of Honor, and local organizations, we give back to our community and beyond.

Brotherhood Events

From formal dinners to intramural sports, tailgates to brotherhood trips—we build bonds that last a lifetime.

Professional Development

Each brother is paired with an alumni mentor that matches their career goals, plus resume workshops and networking events prepare you for success after college.

Social Excellence

Mixers, date functions, and formals with FSU's top sororities create memories that last forever.

Campus Leadership

Our brothers hold positions in Student Government, IFC, and organizations across campus.

Our Foundation

The Creed of Lambda Chi Alpha

"

We believe in Lambda Chi Alpha, and its traditions, principles and ideals. The crescent is our symbol; pure, high ever growing, and the cross is our guide; denoting service, sacrifice, and even suffering and humiliation before the world, bravely endured if need be, in following that ideal.

May we have faith in Lambda Chi Alpha and passion for its welfare. May we have hope for the future of Lambda Chi Alpha and strength to fight for its teachings. May we have pure hearts, that we may approach the ideal of perfect brotherly love.
