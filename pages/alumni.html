Legends of Zeta Rho

Notable Alumni

Brothers who have achieved greatness in their fields while upholding Lambda Chi Alpha values.

Fred Biletnikoff

NFL Hall of Fame

Two-time All-American at FSU, Super Bowl XI MVP with the Oakland Raiders, and Pro Football Hall of Fame inductee. The Biletnikoff Award for the nation's best college receiver bears his name.

Robert Urich

Award-Winning Actor

Star of over 15 television series including Vega$, Spenser: For Hire, and The Lazarus Man. One of the most prolific television actors of his generation.

Ron Sellers

FSU & NFL Legend

Three-time All-American wide receiver at FSU who still holds multiple school records. NFL first-round draft pick and Pro Bowl selection with the Boston Patriots.

Guidance for the Next Generation

Alumni Career Mentors

Our accomplished alumni are ready to help current brothers navigate their careers. These mentors represent decades of experience across diverse industries.

Capt. Pete Douglas

B.S. Finance

Former U.S. Navy jet fighter pilot and leader of the Navy's Top Gun fighter pilot training program. Followed by a 31-year career as a commercial pilot for Eastern and American Airlines. Four-year active chapter member.

Steve Haley

B.S. Hotel & Restaurant Administration

32 years as a General Manager with Hilton Hotels. Currently manages a 468-room Hilton Hotel and Convention Center in Dallas. Linebacker for LXA's Fraternity Intramural Champion Football Team.

Randy Guemple

B.S. Accounting

Retired Florida commercial banker and accountant. Civic leader serving on community boards including housing for university scholarship recipients. Former Executive Director of the Leon County Educational Facilities Authority. Served as Chair and Treasurer of the LXA House Corporation. FSU Baseball pitcher with the record for lowest ERA. Winner of Lambda Chi Alpha's Order of Merit, its highest honor.

Mike Butler

B.S. Finance

45-year commercial banker in leadership positions with Southeast Bank, SunTrust Bank, JP Morgan Chase, and SouthState Bank. Performer in FSU's Flying High Circus who believes in providing "The Greatest Banking Show on Earth." Four-year active chapter member.

Rick Baker

B.A., M.B.A., J.D. from FSU

35-year attorney in the St. Pete Bay area focusing on commercial development and finance. Mayor of St. Petersburg for 10 years, credited with the city's redevelopment. Named America's Mayor of the Year. Author of best-selling Florida history book "Beyond the Sunshine." Former High Alpha and center for LXA's Fraternity Intramural Champion Basketball Team.

Warner Peacock

B.A. Accounting

After serving as accountant for the State of Florida, built a career of over 40 years in management and ownership of multiple retail automobile dealerships in Florida, Georgia, and South Carolina. Investor and developer in banking, multi-family housing, and commercial properties. 2025-2026 President of Seminole Boosters, Inc. Four-year LXA member and name donor of LXA's Peacock Hall.

Tom Smith

B.A. History, MBA Hospital Administration, J.D.

Administrator of major Florida hospitals for 7 years before earning his law degree from South Carolina. 39-year health care attorney in Tampa Bay area. Former High Kappa for LXA Chapter.

Steve Powell

B.A. Government, J.D. Georgetown

FSU's youngest-ever Student Body President, elected as a sophomore. Four-year active chapter member. 39-year attorney for Florida's largest law firm and the City of Jacksonville.

Scott Hillman

B.S. Business Administration

37 years as President of the largest residential real estate firm in Winter Park. Former High Tau for LXA at FSU.

Chuck Kelley

B.S. Accounting

40-year career as owner and manager of family-owned retail grocery and liquor stores in West Florida. Served as Okaloosa County School Board Member and Niceville City Councilman. 38-year FSU Football season ticket holder.

Bill Stropp

B.S. Education

45-year career as a Financial Advisor specializing in advice to U.S. military servicemen and women. Former High Jock for LXA and power forward on the Fraternity Intramural Champion Basketball Team.

Col. Steve German

B.S. Government

30-year career as Officer and Pilot in the U.S. Air Force. Commander of the National Capital Region, serving the White House, National Security Council, NSA, CIA, and State Department. Commanded the 747 military aircraft designated to fly the President during wartime. Former High Alpha for LXA at FSU.

Randy Parrish

B.A. Psychology

38-year career in sales and ownership of commercial lighting companies in the Atlanta area. Two-time successful High Delta for LXA and shooting guard for Fraternity Intramural Champion Basketball Team.

Bruce Blackwell

B.A., J.D. from FSU

United States Air Force Officer. 32-year career in complex civil litigation in Florida, then President/CEO of the Florida Bar Foundation. Active in FSU Student Government and four-year LXA member.

Doug Mannheimer

B.A. Government, J.D.

45-year career in Florida government, health care, and administrative law. National Championship President of Seminole Boosters and Chair of FSU's Athletics Hall of Fame Committee. Former LXA House Corp. President, High Delta, High Rho, and Student Senate President.

Keith Davidson

B.A. Accounting

CPA with a major accounting firm, residential developer, and partner/CFO for a Georgia-based company that develops, owns, and manages apartment communities throughout Georgia. Four-year LXA member.

Dave Dotherow

B.S. Finance

40 years in commercial banking in Florida, including 23 years as CEO of a private bank in Winter Park. Four-year member of LXA at FSU.

Ron Sellers

B.S. Risk Management

One of Florida's long-time leaders in employee benefits advice and insurance for businesses and individuals. Member of the College Football Hall of Fame with retired jersey #34 at Florida State. Caught more passes for more yardage than anyone in college football history at graduation. NFL first-round draft pick by the Patriots and 7-year NFL veteran.

Bob Kirk

B.S. Political Science & Communications, J.D. UF

36 years in law practice, including 30 years as VP of Claims and Litigation for Carnival Cruise Lines. Currently a Certified Mediator. Four-year LXA undergraduate member.

Bill Capko

B.S. Government & History

40-year partner in a major Florida law firm specializing in counsel and issuance of government bonding. Four-year LXA member and former High Jock. Long-time LXA and FSU supporter.

Greg Lawrence

B.S. FSU Magna Cum Laude, J.D. Stetson

35 years experience in commercial banking and real estate law with a major Orlando law firm. Long-time supporter of the LXA Chapter.

James Pyle

B.S. Finance

CEO of one of the South's most successful multi-family development and construction organizations, based in Jacksonville. Four-year undergraduate LXA member and part of a family of three FSU-LXA brothers.

Mike Kelley

B.S. Accounting

Accountant with a major accounting firm. Over 35 years experience as owner/manager of one of Northwest Florida's largest wine and spirits retailers. Former High Tau and avid sailing enthusiast.

Dave Roberts

B.S. & M.S. Communications, J.D. Texas

One of Florida's most experienced employment law experts. Four-year FSU Football letterman, Academic All-American, and NCAA Post-Graduate Scholarship Winner. LXA Chapter Social Chairman and owner/investor in Collegetown's Bowden's.

Dr. Jeff Locke

B.S. Biology, O.D. UAB

Recently retired after 38 years as owner of his private optometry practice in Melbourne, Florida. Four-year active brother and Fraternity Intramural Champion at FSU-LXA.
