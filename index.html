Our Legacy

A Brotherhood Built on Excellence

Lambda Chi Alpha returns to Florida State University with a renewed commitment to our founding principles. The Zeta Rho Chapter has a storied history of producing exceptional leaders, including four FSU Student Body Presidents and celebrated athletes like Fred Biletnikoff and Ron Sellers.

Our brotherhood is founded on the Seven Core Values: Loyalty, Duty, Respect, Service, Honor, Integrity, and Personal Courage. These aren't just words, they're the foundation of everything we do.

Making a Difference

Our Commitment to Service

Movember Foundation is Lambda Chi Alpha's national philanthropy. Movember is a leading charity changing the face of men's health, focusing on mental health and suicide prevention, prostate cancer, and testicular cancer. Each November, brothers across the country grow mustaches to raise awareness and funds for these critical men's health issues.

Folds of Honor is our chapter's local philanthropy. This organization provides educational scholarships to the spouses and children of America's fallen and disabled service members. Through Folds of Honor, we honor the sacrifice of military families right here in our community.
