Florida State University • Zeta-Rho Zeta Alumni Newsletter

APRIL 2026

To our Alumni Brothers of Lambda Chi at Florida State,

Formal Weekend — New Orleans

From April 17–19, the chapter traveled to New Orleans for our spring formal weekend. We rented out a private space at Bourbon Heat on Bourbon Street to celebrate the semester together, and it was easily one of the most memorable weekends of the year.

Brothers and dates at Bourbon Heat during formal weekend in New Orleans

Beyond the events themselves, the weekend was a reminder of why brotherhood matters. Sharing a city, a venue, and a few unforgettable nights brought our actives, new initiates, and dates closer than ever, and set the tone for a strong finish to the semester.

Service at Boys Town

On April 25, the chapter spent the day volunteering at Boys Town North Florida, a nonprofit that provides residential care, family support, and behavioral health services for at-risk youth and families across the region. Their mission is centered on giving kids who've faced difficult circumstances the structure, mentorship, and stability they need to thrive.

Brothers gathered after a day of volunteering at Boys Town North Florida

We spent the afternoon playing sports and doing arts and crafts with the kids, and the day turned out to be one of the more rewarding service events we've put together this year. The chapter is already looking forward to building a longer-term relationship with Boys Town and returning for more events in the semesters ahead.

Brother Accomplishments

Congratulations to Brothers Daniel Scruggs (ZP 2679) and High Theta Anthony Felichio (ZP 2669) on being selected as Movember Ambassadors. Movember is one of Lambda Chi Alpha's national philanthropic partners, focused on men's health—mental health, suicide prevention, and prostate and testicular cancer—and being chosen as ambassadors puts Daniel and Anthony at the front of that effort on our campus. It's a meaningful recognition of their leadership and a strong reflection of what the chapter stands for nationally.

Tarokh presenting at IEEE WAMICON 2026

Brother Tarokh Baniadam (ZP 2701) presented his published paper, "Physical-Layer Authentication of Aircraft via Radio-Frequency Fingerprinting of ADS-B Transmissions," at the IEEE WAMICON 2026 conference. His research focuses on improving the cybersecurity of aircraft by using their unique radio-frequency signatures to verify identity in the air—a real contribution to a fast-moving area of aerospace and defense research.

Brother Lucas Landman (ZP 2688) co-authored a published paper applying machine learning to a class of materials called perovskite oxides. His team analyzed thousands of variations to identify what makes them most effective in real-world uses like EV batteries and solar panels, with the broader goal of designing a new, more efficient material from the ground up.

High Rho Joseph Goyette (ZP 2677) was awarded a $1,200 grant from the Jim Moran College of Entrepreneurship for his startup SailMatch, a platform that connects cruise shoppers with competing travel agents to help them secure the best deal on their trip. SailMatch is set to launch later in 2026.

Looking Ahead

April brought the chapter together in some of the best ways possible—through service, brotherhood, and individual achievement. As we close out the spring semester and look toward our return to Heritage Grove this fall, we're grateful for the alumni who continue to invest their time, energy, and support in Zeta-Rho. Thank you for being part of what we're building.

Eternally in ZAX,

The Active Brothers of Lambda Chi at Florida State

MARCH 2026

To our Alumni Brothers of Lambda Chi at Florida State,

Chapter Update

The brothers of Lambda Chi Alpha Zeta-Rho are proud to share another exciting month of growth, achievement, and brotherhood. March brought a milestone initiation class, strong showings in campus involvement, and continued momentum as the chapter moves through the spring semester.

Spring 2026 Initiation

On March 7, the chapter proudly initiated 24 new brothers, welcoming them into the bonds of Lambda Chi Alpha and the Zeta-Rho chapter.

The Spring 2026 initiation class following the ceremony on March 7

This is a tremendous step forward for the chapter, and we are excited to see the contributions these new brothers will make to our fraternity. Their dedication throughout the associate member process has already shown great promise for the future of Zeta-Rho.

Professional Development

Congratulations to Brothers Matthew Brunelli (ZP 2672) and Alex den Boggende (ZP 2709) on securing 10-week summer internships with Power Design, one of the nation's leading electrical contracting firms. Their success reflects the chapter's commitment to professional growth and career readiness, and we are proud to see our brothers pursuing meaningful opportunities beyond the classroom.

Campus Involvement

Brothers after the Alpha Chi Omega Dash for DVA 5K

Our brothers continue to make their mark across campus through competition, leadership, and service.

Brother Davis Head (ZP 2663) placed 2nd overall in Alpha Chi Omega's Dash for DVA 5K, an impressive finish in a competitive field. Brothers Matthew Brunelli (ZP 2672) and Kaden Combs (ZP 2694) also placed in the race, making it a strong showing for Lambda Chi Alpha.

Our High Gamma, Nicolás Lavín (ZP 2675), was selected as one of just 60 FSU students to participate in a mock Constitutional Convention held March 27–28. This prestigious opportunity highlights his academic excellence and leadership within the university community.

High Theta Anthony Felichio (ZP 2669) was elected Vice President of Community and Social Impact for FSU's American Marketing Association, further demonstrating our brothers' dedication to leadership in professional organizations on campus.

Greek Community Recognition

Former High Rho Jack Engelhard (ZP 2670) was inducted into the Order of Omega, a national honor society recognizing outstanding fraternity and sorority members for their scholarship, leadership, and service. This is a well-deserved honor that reflects Jack's dedication to the Greek community at Florida State.

Jack is also running for Pi Phi's Phinest, a philanthropic competition supporting Pi Beta Phi's literacy initiatives. If you'd like to support Jack's campaign, you can make a donation through the link below—after checkout, simply select Jack Engelhard.

Support Jack — Pi Phi's Phinest

Alumni Connection

We are excited to announce the launch of our Zeta-Rho LinkedIn Group, a space for active brothers and alumni to stay connected, share professional opportunities, and support one another's careers. We encourage all alumni to join and help strengthen the professional network within our chapter.

Join the Zeta-Rho LinkedIn Group

Thank you to our alumni for your continued interest, guidance, and support. The chapter looks forward to building on this momentum as we finish out the spring semester strong.

JANUARY 2026

To our Alumni Brothers of Lambda Chi at Florida State,

Chapter Update

The brothers of Lambda Chi Alpha Zeta-Rho are proud to share a strong start to 2026, marked by academic achievement, brotherhood growth, and continued momentum for the chapter.

Academic Excellence

The chapter continues to uphold a high standard of academic excellence. This semester, three brothers earned a place on the President's List: Ethan Whitley, Luke Novak, and Parker Flanagan.

In addition, 19 brothers were recognized on the Dean's List:

Tin Long Cheng, Wyatt Prater, Ronan Connell, Anthony Felichio, Gavin Conner, Lucas Landman, Julian Mathews, Davis Head, Matthew Brunelli, William Montfort, Will Morrow, Aiden Southern, James Fuller, Ari Cruz, Julian Bueno, Kaden Combs, Cannon Boyles, Nicolás Lavín, and Jack Engelhard.

These achievements reflect the dedication our members continue to show both inside and outside the classroom.

Spring 2026 Rush

Under the leadership of Rush Chair Ayberk Dincturk, the chapter recruited 25 new associate members.

Through strong planning, execution, and brother involvement, this growth marks an exciting step forward as we continue rebuilding and strengthening the Zeta-Rho chapter.

Brotherhood Retreat

Brothers enjoying the Smoky Mountains brotherhood retreat

Brotherhood remained a priority this month. The brothers traveled to the Smoky Mountains for a weekend retreat, staying together in a cabin and exploring Gatlinburg. The trip provided a great opportunity to step away from campus, strengthen bonds, and enjoy time together filled with laughter and shared experiences.

Partnerships & Sponsorships

Brother Tarokh Baniadam worked diligently this semester to secure external partnerships and successfully obtained a sponsorship from Red Bull, which generously supported our Spring 2026 rush events. We are grateful for this partnership and the efforts that made it possible.

Alumni Request

Brother Ari Cruz is currently seeking employment opportunities in the Tallahassee area. If any alumni are local or aware of potential job openings, we would greatly appreciate your support in helping connect him with opportunities. Please reach out to him at ado24@fsu.edu if you can help.

Thank you to our alumni for your continued interest and support. The chapter looks forward to building on this momentum as we move further into 2026.
